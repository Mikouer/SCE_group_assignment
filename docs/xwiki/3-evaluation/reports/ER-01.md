## Method

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Roles | A facilitator introduces the Use Case, its objective and the persona, shows the artifact one step at a time, and takes notes without intervening or explaining problems away. An evaluator steps through the Action Sequence as the persona, answers the questions, justifies the answers and hands the notes to the facilitator. |
| Personas | Path A: Cleber ([ST-01](../../1-foundation/operational-demands/stakeholders/ST-01.md)), who receives the reminders. Path B: Sarah ([HP-01](../../2-specification/personas/human/HP-01.md)), who receives the alert. |
| Steps walked | Path A: UC01 step 2 (the spoken reminder), and steps 4 and 5 (responding, postponing once, the follow-up reminders). Path B: steps 6 and 7 (the unresolved-meal alert and the "resolved" update). Steps 1, 3, 8 and 9 happen inside the system or in the log and are not walkable. |
| Questions at each step | (a) Will the user try to achieve the right effect? (b) Will the user notice that the correct action is available? (c) Will the user associate the correct action with the effect they are trying to achieve? (d) If the correct action is performed, will the user see that progress is being made? Each answer is justified. |
| Recording | A standard form per step: the four answers and justifications, success stories, failure stories, design suggestions and any problem outside the sequence. The evaluator hands the notes to the facilitator. |
| Follow-up questions for CL3 | Open-ended, after the walkthrough: (1) Was there a moment when a reminder felt like too much, or pushy? Which one? (2) Did it feel possible to say no or to postpone, and was it clear how? (3) Would you want Echo to stay quiet at any point? When? Answers are coded as pressure, confusion, none or other, as in [M-03](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-03.md). |
| Material | The prototype [AT-01](../artifacts/AT-01.md), a persona sheet for each path, the Action Sequence and task list, the recording form and the follow-up questions. |

## Summative-only fields

Not applicable: this is a Formative report.

## Evidence produced

None yet. After the session, one Evidence entry is created for each of PR1, PR2, PR3 and CL3. Because the stated Measures are not applied against thresholds in a walkthrough, each entry records the criteria as not assessed and gives its basis: the evaluator's answers and justifications. Each lists as limitations that the evaluator only imagines the persona (which is hard for someone with dementia), that the sample is small, and that the artifact is not a working system.

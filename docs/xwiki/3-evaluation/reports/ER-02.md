## Ethics

Session 1 is a staged technical test with no participants, so no ethics review is required for it.

Session 2 involves people with dementia and their family caregivers. It needs HREC approval.

The planned consent procedure, to be finalised as approved by the HREC:

- **Information and consent.** Plain-language information for each resident and each caregiver. Because the residents have dementia, consent is supported: the resident consents where able, together with the agreement of the family caregiver as proxy, and the resident's assent is checked at the start of each session, treating consent as a process rather than a one-off signature (Dewing, 2007, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/1471301207075625)). Signs of distress or refusal, in words or behaviour, end the observation.
- **Data collected.** The observer's notes and coding, questionnaire answers, interview answers, the system's event log, and the table camera's detections. The camera is limited to the table and processes on the device ([RP-01](../../2-specification/personas/robot/RP-01.md)).
- **Storage, access and retention.** As set out in the HREC application; no retention beyond the project timeline.
- **Withdrawal.** A resident or caregiver can withdraw at any time without penalty, and their data is then removed.

## Introduction

The test addresses all Premises and Claims of [UC01](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01.md): [UC01-F1-PR1](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F1-PR1.md), [UC01-F2-PR2](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-PR2.md), [UC01-F3-PR3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F3-PR3.md), [UC01-F4-PR4](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F4-PR4.md), [UC01-F2-PR5](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-PR5.md) and [UC01-F1-CL1](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F1-CL1.md), [UC01-F2-CL2](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-CL2.md), [UC01-F2-CL3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-CL3.md), [UC01-F3-CL4](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F3-CL4.md), [UC01-F4-CL5](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F4-CL5.md).

**Why the formative check was not enough.** The checks on B1 ran only the prototype's own script, so real sensing and the system-generated log (PR4) were not tested. The walkthrough was the judgement of two evaluators, with nobody playing Sarah. CL1 to CL5 need measured responses from real residents and caregivers.

## Method

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Participants | Session 1: none; researchers run the scenarios. Session 2: a few residents with mild-to-moderate dementia living in assisted-living flats, each with an off-site family caregiver who takes the Sarah role. Recruitment through the facility, as set out in the ethics application. |
| Setting | Assisted-living flats, chosen because the conditions the test needs (kitchen sensing, prepared meals, residents with the relevant needs, an observer on site) can be arranged there. Two differences from the Foundation limit transfer: [SA-01](../../1-foundation/operational-demands/activities/SA-01.md) describes Cleber alone at home, and a family caregiver replaces the professional caregiver Sarah ([ST-03](../../1-foundation/operational-demands/stakeholders/ST-03.md)), who has other clients. |
| Experimental design | Two sessions, Verification before Validation. Session 2 is single-case: each resident is observed with Echo over about a week, with no comparison group. With so few participants the findings are exploratory and not statistically powered. |
| Tasks | Session 1: the researchers run the scripted scenarios of [EM-03](../../1-foundation/human-factors/evaluation-methods/EM-03.md) on B2, including staged alerts. Session 2: residents go about their meals as usual and respond to Echo as they wish (eat, say "not now", or ignore it). The caregiver receives the alert and the visit summary on their phone and says what they would do. |
| Measures | [M-04](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-04.md) (log against the specified sequence); [M-06](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-06.md) (sensor and camera detection against an observer's ground truth); [M-07](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-07.md) (alert delivery time); [M-01](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-01.md) and [M-02](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-02.md) (meal completion and reminder success, logged by the observer); [M-03](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-03.md) (perceived autonomy); [M-05](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-05.md) (clarity of the alert and the summary). |
| Procedure | The protocol is first piloted on one colleague. Session 1: the researchers run the scenarios and compare the event log with the specified Action Sequence; alerts are staged to time their delivery. Session 2: Echo is introduced to each resident and caregiver; an observer follows one or two meals a day for about a week and notes everything, including actual consumption; the caregiver receives alerts and summaries on their phone; afterwards the resident and the caregiver are each interviewed and answer their questionnaire (M-03 is read aloud to the resident). |
| Material | Build B2 with sensing, speakers and the table camera; the caregiver's phone with alerts and summaries; questionnaire and observation sheets; ground-truth record forms; information and consent forms. |
| Analysis | Results are reported per Premise and Claim against the criteria on their pages, descriptively. M-04: scenarios passed, failed or not applicable per rule, and every deviation. M-06: correct, false and missed confirmations and unknowns, counted separately. M-07: elapsed seconds per alert. M-02: initial-reminder success and follow-up rescue rates. M-03: mean and item scores, with the open comment coded. M-05: Part A and Part B means, accuracy and the coded action. The observer's notes on avoidance, startle or confusion are reported with them. |

## Expected outcomes

The thresholds are the starting targets on the Premise and Claim pages, to be confirmed before the session.

| Premise or Claim | Supported if |
| --- | --- |
| PR1 to PR4 (M-04) | Every defined scenario passes: correct events, order and timing, and every rule is exercised at least once. |
| PR3 delivery (M-07) | Alerts arrive within a delivery requirement agreed before testing. |
| PR5 (M-06) | Camera-confirmed consumption matches the observer's record; thresholds for correct, false and missed confirmations are agreed before testing. |
| CL1 and CL2 (M-02) | At least half of first reminders lead to eating, and at least half of missed first reminders are rescued by a follow-up. |
| CL3 (M-03) | Mean of at least 4 out of 5 and no avoidance of Echo observed. |
| CL4 (M-05 Part A) | Part A mean of at least 4, the correct meal and reason in every alert, and a follow-up action stated. |
| CL5 (M-05 Part B) | Part B mean of at least 4, and the unresolved meal and the data gap identified. |

## Limitations

- Small, exploratory sample over a short observation of days; not statistically powered.
- Assisted living, not a private home; staff nearby may change behaviour. A family caregiver stands in for the professional caregiver Sarah.
- M-03 may be hard for some residents to complete.
- PR5 is checked against ground truth on the meals that also serve to validate the Claims, so a Claim may rest on a Premise not yet verified; this is carried into the limitations of the affected Evidence.
- Long-term acceptance in everyday life is not shown.

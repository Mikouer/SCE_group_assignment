## Simulation notice

The source describes spoken prompts, LED/screen behaviour and the escalation
message as live prototype behaviours. A tester stands in for Cleber, and the
trigger used to exercise escalation is simulated.

Its meal-detection description is inconsistent: one paragraph mentions real
appliance/camera sensing, while [B1](AT-01.md#builds) explicitly describes a simulated meal
trigger. The source also states that real-kitchen sensor accuracy cannot be
verified by this build. Treat detection as unverified until the build setup
and ground truth are documented; do not describe a simulated trigger as proof
of real consumption detection.

## Premises verifiable

The build can be used to inspect delivery and the reminder/escalation flow
under [UC01-F1-PR1](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F1-PR1.md), [UC01-F2-PR2](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-PR2.md) and [UC01-F3-PR3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F3-PR3.md), subject to the specific build's
implemented features. [B1](AT-01.md#builds) lacks escalation logic, so it cannot establish the
complete escalation premise.

The source's questions about whether gentle reminders are satisfactory and
preserve autonomy concern claims, particularly [UC01-F2-CL3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-CL3.md), rather than proof
that a function operates as specified. A tester role-play does not validate
those effects for a person with dementia.

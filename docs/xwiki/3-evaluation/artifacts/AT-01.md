## Simulation notice

The prototype simulates Echo and the messages Sarah receives. The facilitator presents the next step according to what the evaluator says the persona would do. Nothing senses, decides or logs anything: the sensors, the camera, the timing and the choice of the next step are all played by the facilitator. The artifact therefore shows what the persona would see and hear, not how a real system behaves.

## Premises verifiable

With B1, [UC01-F1-PR1](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F1-PR1.md), [UC01-F2-PR2](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-PR2.md) and [UC01-F3-PR3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F3-PR3.md) can be checked only for their human-facing steps, as the evaluators' judgement of whether the step works as specified. Timing, sensing, the camera behaviour and the log are assumed, not tested. [UC01-F4-PR4](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F4-PR4.md) cannot be checked. Of the Claims, only [UC01-F2-CL3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-CL3.md) is probed, through open follow-up questions.

## Simulation notice

The prototype simulates the meal-state input and the sensors. A facilitator selects predefined events, such as kitchen entry, eating
confirmed, refusal, no response, plate leaving and returning, or a sensor offline, and the prototype then runs the corresponding spoken
prompts, camera-state display, alerts, log entries and summary. A person plays Cleber and another plays Sarah.

It does not simulate or validate real meal detection, appliance or camera sensor accuracy, speaker coverage in a real home, or the
effects on a person with dementia. Because the facilitator supplies the meal-state events, the detection step is assumed rather than
tested.

## Premises verifiable

With build B2 the scripted scenarios can verify [UC01-F1-PR1](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F1-PR1.md),
[UC01-F2-PR2](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-PR2.md), [UC01-F3-PR3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F3-PR3.md) and
[UC01-F4-PR4](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F4-PR4.md) as conformity to the specified behaviour, not as accuracy of real
sensing. Real meal detection, sensor accuracy and speaker reach are assumed. Whether the reminders improve eating or preserve autonomy
is not a premise: it is evaluated as Claims in [ER-02](../reports/ER-02.md) with stand-in participants.

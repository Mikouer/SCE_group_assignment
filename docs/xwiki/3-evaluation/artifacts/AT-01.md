## Simulation notice

The source describes spoken prompts, LED/screen behaviour and the escalation
message as live prototype behaviours. The online prototype simulates the reminder and escalation flow using scripted scenarios. Participants role-play Cleber and Sarah; a facilitator selects scenario events, such as eating confirmed, refusal, or no response. The prototype displays the corresponding prompts and, in B2, Sarah notification. It does not simulate or validate real meal detection, appliance or camera sensor accuracy, or actual effects on a person with dementia. |

Detection in unverified until the build setup and ground truth are documented; a simulated trigger does not replace proof of real consumption detection.

## Premises verifiable

The selected build can verify whether the prototype displays the intended check-in and reminder sequence for scripted inputs under [UC01-F1-PR1](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F1-PR1.md) and [UC01-F2-PR2](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-PR2.md). B2 can also verify the scripted unresolved-meal notification under [UC01-F3-PR3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F3-PR3.md). Real meal detection, sensor accuracy, and whether reminders preserve autonomy or improve eating remain assumed and are not testable with this simulation. |

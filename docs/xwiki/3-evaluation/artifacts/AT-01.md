# AT-01: Echo meal check-in prototype

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | AT-01 |
| Name | Echo meal check-in prototype |
| Status | In Evaluation. |
| Scope | Build [B1](AT-01.md#builds) is a prototype that represents the steps of [UC01](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01.md) that a person sees or hears: Echo's spoken reminder and its light and head movements, the choices of the persona (go to the kitchen, say "not now" at reminders 1 and 2 only, do nothing), the follow-up reminders (at most five in a scenario: one postponement restarts the sequence of three), the restart after one postponement, Sarah's unresolved-meal alert and the "resolved" update. A side panel lists the scenarios to walk (twelve for Cleber, four for Sarah) and records which were completed. ## Simulation notice

The prototype simulates Echo and the messages Sarah receives. The facilitator presents the next step according to what the evaluator says the persona would do. Nothing senses anything: the sensors, the camera and the timing are played by the facilitator and the evaluators' choices. The prototype does keep a log of the events it plays (reminders, responses, postponement, kitchen entry, eating state, escalation, plate left or returned, sensor offline) and checks it against the rules of [M-04](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-04.md), but that log comes from scripted logic. The artifact therefore shows what the persona would see and hear, and that the scripted logic follows the specified rules, not how a real system behaves. In B1 and B1.1 Cleber saying "I'm done" confirms eating, whereas the specification now requires camera-observed consumption ([UC01](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01.md), step 4); the prototype does not enforce this.

## Premises verifiable

With B1, [UC01-F1-PR1](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F1-PR1.md), [UC01-F2-PR2](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-PR2.md) and [UC01-F3-PR3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F3-PR3.md) can be checked for their human-facing steps, as the evaluators' judgement of whether the step works as specified, and the conformance check applies the [M-04](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-04.md) rules to the log of each scenario. B1 supplies the prerequisites of PR1–PR3: the untouched-meal trigger, camera consumption/meal-end events and Sarah's displayed alert. It does not verify real sensing, timing or delivery to Sarah's receiving interface. [UC01-F4-PR4](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F4-PR4.md) can be checked only against the simulated log and summary (the specified events are logged, plate-away times over one hour are listed first, an offline sensor shows as a data gap); it says nothing about a real record. With B2 (planned), the sensing prerequisites of PR1 to PR3 and [UC01-F2-PR5](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-PR5.md) become verifiable.

Of the Claims, [UC01-F2-CL3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-CL3.md) is probed through open follow-up questions for the person playing Cleber.

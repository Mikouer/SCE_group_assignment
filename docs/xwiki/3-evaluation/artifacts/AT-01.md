## Simulation notice

The prototype simulates Echo and the messages Sarah receives. The facilitator presents the next step according to what the evaluator says the persona would do. Nothing senses anything: the sensors, the camera and the timing are played by the facilitator and the evaluators' choices. The prototype does keep a log of the events it plays (reminders, responses, postponement, kitchen entry, eating state, escalation, plate left or returned, sensor offline) and checks it against the rules of [M-04](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-04.md), but that log comes from scripted logic. The artifact therefore shows what the persona would see and hear, and that the scripted logic follows the specified rules, not how a real system behaves.

## Premises verifiable

With B1, [UC01-F1-PR1](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F1-PR1.md), [UC01-F2-PR2](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-PR2.md) and [UC01-F3-PR3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F3-PR3.md) can be checked for their human-facing steps, as the evaluators' judgement of whether the step works as specified, and the conformance check applies the [M-04](../../1-foundation/human-factors/measures/M-04.md) rules to the log of each scenario. Real timing, sensing and camera behaviour are assumed, not tested. [UC01-F4-PR4](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F4-PR4.md) can be checked only against the simulated log and summary (the specified events are logged, plate-away times over one hour are listed first, an offline sensor shows as a data gap); it says nothing about a real record.

Of the Claims, [UC01-F2-CL3](../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-CL3.md) is probed through open follow-up questions for the person playing Cleber.

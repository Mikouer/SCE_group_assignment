# RP-01: Echo

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | RP-01 |
| Name | Echo |
| Status | Finalized in the source document. |
| Evidence basis | Intended design profile, not independently demonstrated capabilities. |
| Role in Scenario | Kitchen-based social-robot companion; not a mobility aid, a general conversational agent or a replacement for Sarah's visits. |
| Realizes | The selected approach in [TECH-02](../../../1-foundation/technology/TECH-02.md). |

## Social competencies

Echo offers a brief spoken check-in and adaptive responses to speech, taps
or non-response. A soft head turn precedes speech. It has no extended conversation
or small talk. [IDP-01](../../interaction-design-patterns/IDP-01.md) limits a meal episode to an initial prompt and at most
two follow-up reminders.

## Embodiment

A small, owl-like tabletop form with a soft-touch shell, an orientable head/body
and two soft LED eyes. It can orient in place but is not mobile between rooms.
The proposed camera is limited to the kitchen/fridge area, not bedrooms or
bathrooms. Hardware and sensing accuracy are not established by this profile.

## Narrative and identity framing

"A quiet reminder, not a monitor." Echo is deliberately not framed as a safety
device or supervision tool. The first prompt gives Cleber an opportunity to
self-manage, serving achievement ([HV-02](../../../1-foundation/human-factors/human-values/HV-02.md)), before involving Sarah.

## Communication strategies

Soft LEDs brighten and Echo turns slowly towards Cleber before asking whether
he has eaten. Follow-ups remain gentle, without louder speech or indefinite
repetition. Once eating is confirmed or escalation has occurred, it stops
prompting and returns to its resting posture.

## Privacy and data handling

The intended design uses on-device processing. Sarah receives meal-timing
summaries and non-urgent event counts, plus a direct alert when agreed escalation
conditions are met. The source intends no retention beyond the project timeline;
exact retention duration, access controls and consent arrangements still need
to be specified.

## Context-aware capabilities

The intended profile describes softer speech, dimmer LEDs and slower movement
at night, and more present behaviour in daytime kitchen use. It also proposes
adapting urgency to meal supply and Sarah's last visit. These behaviours are not
implemented in [AT-01](../../../3-evaluation/artifacts/AT-01.md) and must not be presented as verified prototype capabilities.

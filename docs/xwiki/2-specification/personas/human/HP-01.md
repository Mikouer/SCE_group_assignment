## Specific capabilities and needs

Sarah is mobile and can travel to Cleber's home, but her time is shared across
multiple clients. She is not continuously on standby and cannot always respond
immediately outside scheduled visits. She needs alerts and summaries she can
receive remotely, rather than having to be present to notice a problem.

She can interpret system information and decide whether an issue warrants
a visit, a phone call, or attention at her next stop.

## Values and motivations

Achievement ([HV-02](../../../1-foundation/human-factors/human-values/HV-02.md)) motivates helping Cleber become more autonomous and less
dependent on her services. Benevolence ([HV-03](../../../1-foundation/human-factors/human-values/HV-03.md)) motivates concern for his health
and continued involvement. Security ([HV-01](../../../1-foundation/human-factors/human-values/HV-01.md)) and health ([HV-04](../../../1-foundation/human-factors/human-values/HV-04.md)) are also relevant.
These concerns can pull in different directions.

## Social network and relationships

Sarah is Cleber's caregiver, visiting every two to three days to prepare meals
and check on him. She has met Gilbert ([ST-02](../../../1-foundation/operational-demands/stakeholders/ST-02.md)), his grandson and principal
long-distance contact. Gilbert relies on her information because he lives in
another city. Sarah lives locally but also cares for other clients.

## History and experiences

On more than one visit she has found prepared meals partly eaten or untouched,
as described in [PS-01](../../../1-foundation/operational-demands/problem-scenarios/PS-01.md). She wants to know what happens between visits rather
than discover a problem only after it has become a repeated pattern.

## Constraints and privacy considerations

She wants alerts for genuinely concerning unresolved situations, not alarms
for every delayed meal. Non-urgent patterns should wait for a visit or summary.
She also wants to avoid making Cleber feel watched, which could damage trust.

The role and task description above corrects source text that incorrectly
described Sarah as the person receiving Echo's meal check-in.

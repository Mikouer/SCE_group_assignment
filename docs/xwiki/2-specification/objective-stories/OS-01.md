## Narrative

Echo's opt-in check-in serves achievement by giving Cleber the first opportunity
to respond and eat on his own before Sarah is informed. [PS-01](../../1-foundation/operational-demands/problem-scenarios/PS-01.md) shows why earlier
support matters: Sarah currently discovers unfinished meals on a later visit,
after a pattern has developed rather than at a moment Cleber could address.

The private-reminder-first approach keeps control with Cleber for as long as
appropriate. Getting escalation timing right matters: involving Sarah too early
can undermine achievement, whereas waiting too long risks leaving a concerning
meal situation unresolved.

The source swapped the security and achievement identifiers in this narrative.
They are aligned here with their value definitions. Its additional `HVF1`
reference had no definition or cited safety floor; no replacement floor or
numeric safety limit has been invented.

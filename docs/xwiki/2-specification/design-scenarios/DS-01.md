## Episode 1: A delayed lunch

It is Wednesday afternoon. Sarah has left prepared food for Cleber, but he has
not been near the kitchen. In the source's illustrative scenario, an absence of
fridge or microwave activity triggers a reminder.

A soft sound comes from the kitchen, followed by a calm invitation:
"Cleber, have you had lunch yet? Come warm up your plate when you are ready."
Cleber is sleeping in his chair and does not respond. Ten minutes later, another
gentle reminder tells him that lunch is in the fridge. He wakes, goes to the
kitchen and heats the meal.

The system ends reminders once eating is confirmed. The response delay is kept
for Sarah's next visit summary rather than generating an urgent alert. The
source originally inferred consumption from kitchen/plate activity; [F2](../use-cases/uc01/F2.md) requires
the stricter distinction between activity and confirmed eating.

## Episode 2: An unresolved breakfast

On the following day, the breakfast reminder is triggered. There is no confirmed
eating after the three-reminder sequence. Instead of continuing indefinitely,
the system sends Sarah an unresolved-meal notification, and she calls Cleber
rather than waiting until her next visit.

The source uses two-hour, ten-minute and one-hour intervals illustratively.
It also gives inconsistent elapsed times for the lunch episode. Those timings
are not validated safety thresholds; the agreed escalation conditions remain
an open design issue in [VT-01](../../1-foundation/human-factors/value-tensions/VT-01.md).

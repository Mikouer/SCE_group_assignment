## Episode 1: A delayed lunch

It is Wednesday afternoon, the planned lunchtime. Sarah has left prepared food for
Cleber, and the appliance sensors show that the meal is still untouched.

Echo plays a soft sound on the speaker in the room where Cleber is resting,
followed by a calm invitation: "Cleber, have you had lunch yet? Come warm up your
plate when you are ready." Cleber is asleep in his chair and does not respond.
About ten minutes later Echo gently reminds him that lunch is in the fridge. He
wakes, goes to the kitchen and heats the meal.

Echo stops reminding him only once eating is confirmed ([F2](../use-cases/uc01/F2.md));
going to the kitchen alone does not count. The delay before he responded is not
sent as an alert. It is kept for Sarah's next visit summary ([F4](../use-cases/uc01/F4.md)).

## Episode 2: An unresolved breakfast

The next morning the breakfast reminder starts. After three gentle reminders,
eating is still not confirmed. Instead of continuing indefinitely, Echo sends Sarah
an unresolved-meal notification that says which meal it is and whether Cleber
declined or did not respond ([F3](../use-cases/uc01/F3.md)). She calls Cleber
rather than waiting for her next visit. If he then eats, Echo sends her a short
"resolved" update.

The times in these episodes (about ten minutes between reminders, a sequence of
roughly an hour) are illustrative starting values to be tuned
([TECH-02](../../1-foundation/technology/TECH-02.md)). They are not validated safety
thresholds; the agreed escalation conditions remain an open design issue in
[VT-01](../../1-foundation/human-factors/value-tensions/VT-01.md).

# EM-02: Post-interaction questionnaire and interview

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | EM-02 |
| Name | Post-interaction questionnaire and interview |
| Status | In Progress |
| Evidence basis | Candidate validation method for perceived autonomy, acceptance and caregiver understanding, planned for the summative test (ER-02, Session 2); no completed study is cited. The questionnaires are our own adaptations (see [M-03](../measures/M-03.md) and [M-05](../measures/M-05.md)). |
| Procedure summary | After the observation week, the facilitator reads the short Likert questionnaire to the resident ([M-03](../measures/M-03.md)). The resident's family caregiver, who takes the Sarah role, answers a questionnaire after receiving the unresolved-meal alert and the visit summary on their phone ([M-05](../measures/M-05.md)). Each then gets a few open questions in a post-interview, and the observer notes behaviour such as avoiding, ignoring or disabling Echo. |
| Purpose | Validation. |
| Human subjects required | Yes: residents with mild-to-moderate dementia and their family caregivers. |
| Measures used | [M-03](../measures/M-03.md) and [M-05](../measures/M-05.md). |
| Cost and feasibility | Low: a few minutes per participant, plus consent and a facilitator. |
| Known limitations | The questionnaires are unvalidated adaptations, and answers can be affected by wanting to please the evaluators. Dementia may affect questionnaire completion, so the questions are read aloud and complemented by observation. |

[HFC-02](../concepts/HFC-02.md) and the research linked in [IDP-01](../../../2-specification/interaction-design-patterns/IDP-01.md) motivate attention to agency; they do
not validate the questionnaires.

Not yet applied: the Likert questionnaires are planned for ER-02 (Session 2). The open question of [M-03](../measures/M-03.md) was used in the walkthrough ([EM-04](EM-04.md)).

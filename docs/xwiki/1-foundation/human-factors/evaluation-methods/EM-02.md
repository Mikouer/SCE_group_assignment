# EM-02: Post-interaction questionnaire and interview

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | EM-02 |
| Name | Post-interaction questionnaire and interview |
| Status | In Progress |
| Evidence basis | Candidate formative method for perceived autonomy, acceptance and caregiver understanding; no completed study is supplied. The questionnaires are our own adaptations (see [M-03](../measures/M-03.md) and [M-05](../measures/M-05.md)). |
| Procedure summary | After the meal-support session, the facilitator reads the short Likert questionnaire to the person playing Cleber ([M-03](../measures/M-03.md)). The person playing Sarah answers her questionnaire after receiving the unresolved-meal alert and the visit summary ([M-05](../measures/M-05.md)). Each then gets a few open questions in an informal interview, and the observer notes behaviour such as avoiding, ignoring or disabling Echo. |
| Purpose | Validation. |
| Human subjects required | Yes: stand-in participants for Cleber and Sarah. |
| Measures used | [M-03](../measures/M-03.md) and [M-05](../measures/M-05.md). |
| Cost and feasibility | Low: a few minutes per participant, plus consent and a facilitator. |
| Known limitations | Answers from stand-ins cannot establish Cleber's or a real caregiver's experience. The questionnaires are unvalidated adaptations, and answers can be affected by wanting to please the evaluators. Dementia may affect questionnaire completion in a later study, so oral administration and observation would be needed. |

[HFC-02](../concepts/HFC-02.md) and the research linked in [IDP-01](../../../2-specification/interaction-design-patterns/IDP-01.md) motivate attention to agency; they do
not validate the questionnaires.

Not applied in the current cycle: its Likert questionnaire is kept for a later session. The open question of [M-03](../measures/M-03.md) is used in the walkthrough ([EM-04](EM-04.md)).

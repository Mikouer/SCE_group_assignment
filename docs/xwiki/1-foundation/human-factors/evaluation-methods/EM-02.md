# EM-02: Post-interaction interview and observation

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | EM-02 |
| Name | Post-interaction interview and behavioural observation |
| Status | In Progress |
| Evidence basis | Candidate formative method for perceived autonomy and acceptance; no completed study is supplied. |
| Procedure summary | After an interaction, ask accessible questions about acceptance and perceived choice, and combine responses with behavioural observation. The proposed questionnaire remains unspecified. |
| Purpose | Validation. |
| Human subjects required | Yes. |
| Measures used | [M-03](../measures/M-03.md). |
| Cost and feasibility | Low in the source's estimate, subject to consent, suitable facilitation and an appropriate instrument. |
| Known limitations | Dementia may affect questionnaire completion. Oral interviews and observation may be needed, and proxy or tester responses cannot establish Cleber's own experience. |

[HFC-02](../concepts/HFC-02.md) and the research linked in [IDP-01](../../../2-specification/interaction-design-patterns/IDP-01.md) motivate attention to agency; they do
not validate the proposed questionnaire.

# EM-01: Structured observation of simulated meal-support sessions

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | EM-01 |
| Name | Structured observation of simulated meal-support sessions |
| Status | In Progress |
| Evidence basis | Validation method based on [SA-01](../../operational-demands/activities/SA-01.md). It is planned for the summative test ([ER-02](../../../3-evaluation/reports/ER-02.md), Session 2) with real meals; no validation study is cited. |
| Procedure summary | Residents with mild-to-moderate dementia use Echo ([AT-01](../../../3-evaluation/artifacts/AT-01.md), build B2 with sensing) in their assisted-living flats, and an observer follows one or two meals a day for about a week per resident. Nothing is scripted. For each reminder the observer codes whether the resident notices it, starts the eating step, refuses or postpones, or does not respond, records the interaction flow, notes any startle or confusion, and notes actual meal consumption. |
| Purpose | Validation of the reminder Claims (the effect of the reminders on responses and on eating) with a small group of residents. |
| Human subjects required | Yes: residents with mild-to-moderate dementia, each with an off-site family caregiver. |
| Measures used | [M-01](../measures/M-01.md) and [M-02](../measures/M-02.md). |
| Cost and feasibility | Moderate: consent from residents and caregivers, an observer at the observed meals, a facilitator and a build with sensing. |
| Known limitations | The sample is small and exploratory and the observation lasts days, so long-term use is not shown. Staff nearby in assisted living may change behaviour, and the observer can influence behaviour. |

Not yet applied: planned for [ER-02](../../../3-evaluation/reports/ER-02.md) (Session 2).

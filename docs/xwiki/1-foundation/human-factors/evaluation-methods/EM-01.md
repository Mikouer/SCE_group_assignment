# EM-01: Structured observation of simulated meal-support sessions

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | EM-01 |
| Name | Structured observation of simulated meal-support sessions |
| Status | In Progress |
| Evidence basis | Candidate formative method based on [SA-01](../../operational-demands/activities/SA-01.md). It replaces the earlier plan to observe a real meal, which is not possible with a role-play prototype; no completed protocol or validation study is supplied. |
| Procedure summary | A person role-playing Cleber goes through scripted meal-support situations with the [AT-01](../../../3-evaluation/artifacts/AT-01.md) prototype (for example asleep in another room, in the kitchen but not hungry, or ready to eat). Each situation is described on a card, but the outcome is not scripted: he responds as he thinks Cleber would. An observer codes, for each reminder, whether he notices it, whether he starts the eating step, refuses or postpones, or does not respond, and records the interaction flow. Real meal consumption is **not** observed. |
| Purpose | Validation of interaction Claims with a stand-in participant (the effect of the reminders on responses); it cannot establish effects on people with dementia. |
| Human subjects required | Yes: the person playing Cleber (a stand-in, not a person with dementia). |
| Measures used | [M-02](../measures/M-02.md). [M-01](../measures/M-01.md) is not used in this cycle because it needs a real observed meal. |
| Cost and feasibility | Low to moderate: two participants, a facilitator, an observer and the prototype. |
| Known limitations | The participant is not representative of a person with dementia, the situations are staged and short, and the observer can influence behaviour. Results show how the interaction works for a stand-in, not long-term behaviour at home. Observing real meals and meal completion remains future work. |

Not applied in the current cycle: it is a candidate for a later role-play session with the prototype.

# HFC-02: Self-management and agency

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | HFC-02 |
| Name | Self-management and agency |
| Status | In Progress; completed from the source's previously citation-only entry. |
| Evidence basis | Dixon, Piper and Lazar's interview study of 17 technology-savvy people with mild to moderate dementia. |
| Concept statement | People with dementia can actively configure technological and social resources to manage their lives and preserve control. Support should recognise this agency rather than presume that all decisions must be delegated to a caregiver. |
| Human factor addressed | Agency, perceived choice and self-management. |
| Proposed mechanism | Letting a person shape and respond to support preserves opportunities for self-directed action. Whether Echo achieves this remains a project hypothesis. |
| Source | Dixon, E., Piper, A. M., and Lazar, A. (2021), ["Taking care of myself as long as I can": How People with Dementia Configure Self-Management Systems](https://doi.org/10.1145/3411764.3445225). The source's institution-proxied PubMed link resolves to [PMID 34250524](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34250524/). |
| Conditions and population of validity | Qualitative findings from a specific technology-savvy, mild-to-moderate dementia sample; not a validated reminder trial, a psychometric instrument, or evidence for all dementia populations. |
| Predicted Effect type | Opportunity for self-directed action and retained perceived control. |
| Associated Measures | [M-03](../measures/M-03.md) is the project's proposed measure; its instrument and scoring remain to be specified and validated. |

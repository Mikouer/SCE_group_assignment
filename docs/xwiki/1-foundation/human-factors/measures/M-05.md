# M-05: Caregiver notification clarity

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | M-05 |
| Name | Caregiver notification clarity |
| Status | In Progress |
| Evidence basis | Bespoke measure derived from [PS-01](../../operational-demands/problem-scenarios/PS-01.md) (Sarah does not notice a skipped meal until her next visit), [HP-01](../../../2-specification/personas/human/HP-01.md) and [HFC-01](../concepts/HFC-01.md) (understandable, appropriate information). |
| Construct measured | Whether Sarah ([ST-03](../../operational-demands/stakeholders/ST-03.md)) understands the unresolved-meal alert and the visit summary well enough to decide what to do. |
| Type | Quantitative (Likert) with coded behaviour. |
| Instrument or procedure | The person playing Sarah receives the scripted unresolved-meal alert (Part A) and the visit summary (Part B), then answers on a 1 to 5 scale (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree). **Part A, alert:** (A1) I understood which meal was missed and how long it had been. (A2) I could tell whether Cleber declined or did not respond. (A3) I knew what I would do next (call, visit or wait). **Part B, summary:** (B1) I could see which meals were eaten and which were not. (B2) The reminder timing and postponement information was understandable. (B3) The plate-away times let me judge whether something was concerning. (B4) Missing data was clearly shown, so I did not read a gap as "all fine". She also states what she would do, which the observer codes as *call*, *visit*, *wait* or *unsure*. |
| Scoring or coding scheme | Mean score for Part A and for Part B separately (1 to 5). Accuracy check: yes or no for whether she names the correct meal and the correct reason (refusal versus no response). Report the coded action. **Proposed target: mean of at least 4 for each part, and correct identification in every scripted alert**; to be confirmed by the team before the session. |
| Validity and reliability | Bespoke and unvalidated. When answered by a person role-playing Sarah, it tests whether the message and report are readable, not how a real caregiver with other clients would react. |
| Serves | Validation. |
| Burden | Low (about three minutes). |

Used by [EM-02](../evaluation-methods/EM-02.md) to examine
[UC01-F3-CL4](../../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F3-CL4.md) (Part A) and
[UC01-F4-CL5](../../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F4-CL5.md) (Part B).

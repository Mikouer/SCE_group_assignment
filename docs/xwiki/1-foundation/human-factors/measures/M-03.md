# M-03: Perceived autonomy

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | M-03 |
| Name | Perceived autonomy |
| Status | In Progress |
| Evidence basis | Bespoke short questionnaire. Its wording is modelled on perceived-choice items from self-determination theory (the theory behind [HV-02](../human-values/HV-02.md) and [HFC-02](../concepts/HFC-02.md)); the items and the 1 to 5 scale used here are our own adaptation, not a validated instrument. |
| Construct measured | Cleber's perceived choice and control while receiving eating-related support from Echo, including his comfort with being reminded again. |
| Type | Quantitative (Likert), with one optional open comment. |
| Instrument or procedure | A five-item questionnaire read aloud by the facilitator after the meal-support session, answered on a 1 to 5 scale (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree): (1) I felt I could decide for myself how to respond to Echo. (2) Echo's reminders felt like an invitation, not an order. (3) I did not feel pressured to eat when Echo spoke. (4) I felt in control of what happened during the reminders. (5) I would be comfortable with Echo reminding me like this again. Then one open question: "Was there a moment when a reminder felt like too much?" The questions are read aloud and kept short so that they remain suitable for a future session with a person with dementia. |
| Scoring or coding scheme | Mean of the five items (1 to 5); higher means greater perceived autonomy. Item scores are also reported, because item 5 (acceptance) can differ from items 1 to 4. The open comment is coded as *pressure*, *confusion*, *none* or *other*. **Proposed target: mean of at least 4**, following the target used in the manual's worked autonomy example; to be confirmed by the team before the session. |
| Validity and reliability | Provisional. Adapted for this project and not validated; [HFC-02](../concepts/HFC-02.md) supports the relevance of agency but does not validate the questionnaire. When answered by a person role-playing Cleber, it measures how the interaction felt to that person, not Cleber's own experience. |
| Serves | Validation. |
| Burden | Low in the current cycle (about two minutes). Moderate in a future session with a person with dementia, which needs consent, accessible administration and attention to cognitive load. |

Used by [EM-02](../evaluation-methods/EM-02.md) to examine [OBJ-02](../../../2-specification/use-cases/OBJ-02.md) and
[UC01-F2-CL3](../../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-CL3.md). The questionnaire should be piloted on one colleague for
comprehension before the session.

In the current cycle only the open question is used, as the follow-up for [UC01-F2-CL3](../../../2-specification/use-cases/uc01/UC01-F2-CL3.md) in the cognitive walkthrough ([EM-04](../evaluation-methods/EM-04.md)). The Likert items are kept for a later session with participants.

**# HV-04: Health**

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | HV-04 |
| Name | Health |
| Status | Finalized |
| Evidence basis | Case description, interpreted with Strikwerda et al. (2022). The risk of malnutrition in dementia is described in the ESPEN guideline (Volkert et al., 2015, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clnu.2015.09.004)). Provisional: no direct contact with the stakeholder group. |
| Situated meaning | For ST-01: Maintaining his health through adequate eating.<br><br>For ST-02: Ensuring ST-01 remains healthy.<br><br>For ST-03: Wants to make sure ST-01 is follows a healthy diet so he doesn’t get sick |
| Held by | ST-01, ST-03, ST-02, all with high priority. |
| Intrinsic or instrumental | Intrinsic. |
| Recognition criteria | Served: ST-01 maintains adequate and regular food intake and his health does not deteriorate.<br><br>Violated: Repeatedly missed meals contribute to deterioration in ST-01's health. |
| Source | Strikwerda, L., van Steenbergen, M., van Gorp, A., Timmers, C., and van Grondelle, J. (2022). [The value sensitive design of a preventive health check app](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10676-022-09662-x). *Ethics and Information Technology*, 24(3). |

[PS-01](../../operational-demands/problem-scenarios/PS-01.md) motivates this concern. [M-01](../measures/M-01.md) can describe meal consumption, but clinical conclusions would require appropriate evidence beyond the current prototype.

## Narrative

Sarah notices that meals she prepared are not being consumed when she returns
to prepare more. Cleber feels weaker and more tired than usual, and the reported
doctor checkup results appear worse than at the preceding visit. The problem is
discovered after a pattern of missed or unfinished meals rather than at the
moment support could have helped.

These are observations and candidate explanations reported by the source,
not a new clinical diagnosis or an independently verified causal finding.

**# PS-01: Missing a planned meal**

*Cleber misses a planned meal.*

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ID | PS-01 |
| Name | Missing a planned meal |
| Status | In Progress |
| Evidence basis | Constructed from [SA-01](../activities/SA-01.md), proxy evidence from Gilbert, evidence from Sarah, and reported checkup results. Provisional. |
| Narrative | It is 12.30 which is the planned mealtime. The meal is already available since Sarah has kept the meal ready before leaving earlier that day. Cleber does not remember to eat. Meal remains untouched even after mealtime has passed. Sarah notices this later when she comes to prepare more meals. Thus the missed meal went unnoticed. |
| Stakeholders involved | Cleber ([ST-01](../stakeholders/ST-01.md)) and Sarah ([ST-03](../stakeholders/ST-03.md)) |
| Activities involved | [SA-01](../activities/SA-01.md). |
| Breakdown point | ST-01 does not start his meal within the meal period that was intended, resulting in delayed or missed meal. |
| Causes | Root cause A; he does not initiate the meal (unintentional): He forgets or does not remember to eat, is in another room and does not notice the planned meal, or sleeps through the alarm or reminder. All of these are considered under the same root cause. Root cause B; he chooses not to eat (intentional): He is not hungry, dislikes the prepared food, or does not feel like eating.|
| Sub-problems | SP-01: ST-01 does not initiate a planned meal<br><br>SP-02: ST-03 does not notice the skipped meal until next visit. |
| Out of scope | Hydration; diagnosis and treatment of appetite-related condition. |

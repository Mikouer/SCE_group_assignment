**# ST-01: Cleber**

| Field | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- |------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| ID | ST-01                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Name | Cleber                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Status | Finalized                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Evidence basis | Case description. The risk of malnutrition in dementia is described in the ESPEN guideline (Volkert et al. [1]) and difficulty with meals in observational work (Jung et al. [2]). Provisional |
| Type | Direct: interacts with Echo.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Values and motivations | Security ([HV-01](../../human-factors/human-values/HV-01.md)) and achievement/independence ([HV-02](../../human-factors/human-values/HV-02.md)) have very high priority. Health ([HV-04](../../human-factors/human-values/HV-04.md)) is also identified. |
| Relationship to other Stakeholders | Grandfather of Gilbert ([ST-02](ST-02.md)), supported by caregiver Sarah ([ST-03](ST-03.md)), and receives routine health checks from Hanz ([ST-04](ST-04.md)).                                                              |

His meal activity is documented in [SA-01](../activities/SA-01.md) and its breakdown in [PS-01](../problem-scenarios/PS-01.md).
